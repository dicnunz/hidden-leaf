# Reconstruction evidence

One metre is one world unit. Blender uses Z up; runtime metadata uses Godot's Y up, with `(x, y, z) → (x, z, -y)` at export.

Geometry claims have three levels:

- **Observed:** visible in an inspected canonical image. This applies to the visible feature, never automatically to its hidden sides or metric size.
- **Constrained:** derived from identified observations. Record the anchors, acceptable range and interpretation.
- **Inferred:** connective geography or construction that the source does not determine. It must remain compatible with the observations.

An episode summary or chapter index is a locator, not proof of the geometry inside that episode or chapter. Images hosted by a secondary site require source and era checks before they can support an observed claim. The public VIZ reader requires membership for the relevant chapters; locating those chapters does not establish that their panels were inspected.

For conflicts, prefer the selected era, then original manga, repeated anime depictions and spatial coherence. Licensed games may constrain otherwise unresolved space, but do not override stronger evidence. Boruto-era redevelopment is excluded. Reference images stay outside the repository and shipping assets.

Absolute dimensions are reconstructions. Door heights of 2.0–2.3 m and floor heights of 2.8–3.6 m are human-scale construction assumptions unless a particular source supplies a stronger anchor. Source-visible ratios constrain silhouettes before dimensions are chosen. No entire village is certified as a literal 1:1 map.

The source register records what was actually inspected and what remains unverified. Art approval requires side-by-side runtime views, including ordinary eye-level traversal, with the corresponding evidence. A valid register does not itself constitute fidelity approval.
